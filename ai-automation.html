How Intake Works

  1. Identify the requester. We capture the person, business, contact method, decision maker, and urgency.
  2. Define the exact request. We document what needs to happen, what outcome is expected, and what should not happen.
  3. Map the current process. We gather current tools, logins needed later, existing forms, email accounts, stores, spreadsheets, and pain points.
  4. Set rules and approvals. We define what AI may draft, what requires human approval, and what actions are never automated without permission.
  5. Create the build file. The intake becomes a structured implementation record for workflow design, pricing, build, testing, and support.
