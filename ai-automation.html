How Intake Works

  1. Identify the requester. Capture the person, business, contact method, decision maker, and urgency.
  2. Define the exact request. Document what needs to happen, what outcome is expected, and what should not happen.
  3. Map tools and connections. Gather current apps, manual steps, integrations, files, forms, accounts, and constraints.
  4. Set rules and approvals. Define what AI may draft, what requires human approval, and what is never automated without permission.
  5. Create the build file. Turn intake into a structured implementation record for pricing, build, testing, launch, and support.
