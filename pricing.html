Website Services for Your Business

Whether you need a new website or help maintaining an existing one, Reed's Solutions, LLC can scope a service around your business goals, content, design preferences, and required functionality.

Project pricing is determined by design, functionality, content needs, and the level of ongoing support selected. Request a consultation for a custom proposal.

How the Website Process Works

  1. Consultation: We discuss your business, goals, existing website, and the type of customers you want to reach.
  2. Project scope: We identify the pages, content, design direction, functionality, and support level required.
  3. Proposal and agreement: You receive a written scope, custom quote, timeline, and service terms for approval.
  4. Onboarding: You provide approved business details, branding, photographs, contact information, and any required access.
  5. Development: We create a private website for your review based on the agreed scope.
  6. Review: You review the website and provide the included revision feedback.
  7. Approval and launch: After written approval and launch authorization, we publish the website and test its key pages and contact paths.
  8. Ongoing management: If selected, the management service begins according to the agreement and includes the stated monthly support allowance.

What Is a Minor Content Update?

Examples include changing business hours or contact details, replacing a customer-supplied image, editing an existing service description, updating an existing link, or revising a short announcement on an existing page.

Monthly update allowances do not roll over. New pages, major redesigns, extensive copywriting, custom software, online stores, advanced booking systems, and new integrations are outside routine minor updates and are quoted separately.

What May Require a Separate Quote?

  • Domain registration and renewal fees.
  • Third-party hosting charges or premium hosting plans.
  • Paid themes, plugins, software, or integrations.
  • Business email services.
  • Professional photography, video, or logo design.
  • E-commerce, online payments, appointment booking, customer portals, or custom applications.
  • Additional pages, major redesigns, or work beyond the selected monthly update allowance.

Any third-party expense or additional work will be identified for your approval before it is incurred.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you publish the website before I approve it?

No. Your website remains a private review project until you approve the final version and authorize publication.

Do I need to provide the website content?

You provide or approve the business facts, services, contact details, photographs, and other information used on the website. We can discuss additional content-development work if needed.

Does website management guarantee search rankings or new customers?

No. Search setup and visibility support help maintain website information and technical basics, but search rankings, traffic, leads, and sales are not guaranteed.

Can you work with my existing domain or website?

Yes. We can review an existing website and domain to determine whether an update, redesign, or new build is the best fit. Access and ownership must be confirmed before changes are made.

When does ongoing management begin?

If you select a management service, its start date, recurring billing, and cancellation terms will be stated in your written agreement. The standard proposed arrangement is to begin management at website launch.
